# Demo Recording Script

## Demo Goal
Record a screen + phone video showing the full outbound lead qualification flow for the portfolio at bitransky.vercel.app.

## Recording Checklist

### Before Recording
- [ ] n8n workflow is working end-to-end
- [ ] Vapi assistant is configured and tested
- [ ] Google Sheets is connected and receiving data
- [ ] Phone is charged and camera ready
- [ ] Screen recording software running

### Demo Flow (Screen Recording)

1. **Show Google Sheets** — empty or with sample data, explain the columns
2. **Show n8n workflow** — walk through the nodes briefly
3. **Submit the form** — fill in test lead data with your PH number
4. **Show n8n executing** — highlight each node lighting up
5. **Switch to phone** — show the incoming call, answer it
6. **Have the AI conversation** — go through the qualification questions
7. **Back to screen** — show Google Sheets updating with structured data
8. **Call out the results** — highlight qualification fields populated by AI

### Talking Points
- "This is an automated outbound lead qualification system"
- "When a lead submits a form, the AI calls them within seconds"
- "No human intervention required — the AI qualifies the lead autonomously"
- "All data is structured and logged for easy pipeline review"
- "Built with n8n for orchestration and Vapi for voice AI"
